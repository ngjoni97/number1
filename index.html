01  /  Services

Everything from a stuck car at 2am to a full modernization.

No job is too small. We take single-car walk-ups and multi-bank high-rises with the same crew and the same paperwork discipline.

01

Emergency repair

Entrapments, shutdowns, door faults, controller problems. We find the actual cause, carry common parts, and get the car back in service instead of resetting it and leaving.

02

Preventive maintenance

A written program built around the age, duty cycle, and condition of your equipment — not a generic monthly visit. You get a service record you can hand to an inspector.

03

Modernization

Controllers, machines, door operators, fixtures, and cab interiors brought up to current code and current expectations. Smoother rides, fewer callbacks, longer equipment life.

04

New installation

Passenger, freight, and residential units in new construction and retrofits. Non-proprietary equipment, so you are never locked into one contractor for parts or software.

05

Testing & inspection

Category 1 and Category 5 testing, witnessing, and filing. We schedule around your building, coordinate with the inspector, and handle submission end to end.

06

Violation removal

Open violations and outstanding items corrected, documented, and cleared. We tell you what it will take before we start, in writing.

02  /  Equipment

If it moves people or freight vertically, we work on it.

Older relay logic through current microprocessor controls. Three drive types cover almost every building we walk into.

Hydraulic

Low-rise workhorses. Jacks, packing, valves, and tank work — plus the leak that nobody wrote up for three years.

Geared traction

Mid-rise machines, brakes, and ropes. Gearbox noise and brake drift are the two calls we get most.

Gearless & MRL

Machine-room-less and gearless traction. Tight overheads, permanent magnet machines, and proprietary tooling we work around.

04  /  How it works

Five steps, no surprises on the invoice.

01

You call or send the details

Address, equipment, and what it is doing. If a car is down or someone is trapped, say so first — that call jumps the queue.

02

We survey the equipment

A mechanic looks at the machine room, the car, and the service history. Site surveys are free, including for buildings currently under contract with someone else.

03

You get a written scope and price

Line-item parts and labor, with the code requirement or failure mode behind each item. If something can wait, we say which things can wait.

04

Licensed mechanics do the work

We schedule around tenants and building access, keep the shaft and lobby clean, and tell your super what is happening each day.

05

Filing and sign-off

Test paperwork, filings, and close-out documentation handled by us. You end up with a file that holds up at inspection time.
